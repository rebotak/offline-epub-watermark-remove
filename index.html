Offline EPUB Watermark Remove

Remove a watermark string from an EPUB — entirely offline, nothing is uploaded.

This offline EPUB watermark remove tool runs 100% in your own browser: your file never leaves your device, there is no server, no account, and no upload. It is made for books you own — cleaning purchase stamps and download-site footers that interfere with personal use, device layout, accessibility tools, or text-to-speech narration. It does not support piracy.
